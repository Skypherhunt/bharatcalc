SIP Calculation Formula

The standard future value of annuity formula — the same one all Indian mutual fund platforms use.

SIP Formula
M = P × { ([1+i]ⁿ − 1) / i } × (1+i)
M = Maturity value
P = Monthly SIP amount
i = Monthly rate (r ÷ 12 ÷ 100)
n = Total months (years × 12)
📈
Monthly compounding
Each installment earns interest from the following month onwards.
⚠️
Estimated, not guaranteed
Mutual fund returns are market-linked. A fixed rate is assumed for illustration.
🧾
Pre-tax returns
LTCG of 10% applies on equity gains above ₹1L/year. Values shown are before tax.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is SIP and how does it work? +

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) lets you invest a fixed amount in a mutual fund every month — automatically debited from your bank. You benefit from rupee cost averaging and the power of compounding over time.

What return rate should I use? +

For large-cap equity funds, 10–12% p.a. is a reasonable historical estimate. Mid/small-cap funds: 12–15%. Debt funds: 6–8%. Always use conservative estimates for long-term planning.

How much should I invest per month? +

A practical rule: invest 20% of your monthly take-home income. Earning ₹50,000/month? A ₹10,000 SIP is a healthy start. Work backwards from your target corpus using the calculator above.

Can I stop or change my SIP anytime? +

Yes — SIP is completely flexible. Pause, reduce, increase (Step-up SIP), or stop it at any time with no penalty. Your existing units remain in your account and continue to grow.

What is Step-up SIP? +

Step-up SIP increases your monthly amount by a fixed % every year (typically 10–15%) to match salary hikes. A ₹5,000 SIP with 10% annual step-up over 20 years produces nearly 2× the corpus of a flat SIP.
