What Is a Unit Converter?

A unit converter changes a measurement from one unit into another within the same category — for example centimetres to inches, kilograms to pounds, or Celsius to Fahrenheit. Most conversions are a simple multiplication by a fixed factor; temperature is the exception because it uses a formula with an offset. This tool covers six everyday categories and updates the result the moment you type.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common unit conversion questions

How do I convert centimetres to inches?
Divide the centimetre value by 2.54, because 1 inch = 2.54 cm. Example: 30 cm ÷ 2.54 = 11.81 inches. Select Length, choose cm and inch, and the tool does it instantly.
How do I convert kilograms to pounds?
Multiply the kilogram value by 2.20462, because 1 kg = 2.20462 pounds. Example: 70 kg × 2.20462 = 154.32 lb. Pick Weight, then kg and pound.
How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?
Use Fahrenheit = (Celsius × 9/5) + 32. Example: 37°C = (37 × 9/5) + 32 = 98.6°F. Temperature is not a simple ratio, so this tool applies the correct formula automatically.
What is the difference between metric and imperial units?
Metric units (metre, kilogram, litre) are based on powers of ten and are the standard in India. Imperial/US units (foot, pound, gallon) are used mainly in the US and UK. This converter moves between both systems in one step.
Is this unit converter free and accurate?
Yes. It is completely free, needs no login, and uses standard internationally agreed conversion factors, so results are accurate for everyday and academic use. Results update live as you type.
