How Age Is Calculated

Your exact age is the gap between your date of birth and a chosen date, broken into years, then months, then days. The calculation respects the real length of each month (28–31 days) and leap years, so it is more accurate than simply subtracting years.

Example

Born 15 Aug 1990, as of 25 Jun 2026:

= 35 years, 10 months, 10 days

In India, official forms almost always use completed years — the age you turned at your last birthday, not your nearest birthday.

Where You Need Your Exact Age

  • →Government exams — UPSC, SSC, banking and state exams set age limits as on a fixed cut-off date.
  • →School admissions — most schools require the child to be a minimum age as on 31 March / 1 June.
  • →Income tax — senior (60+) and super-senior (80+) slabs depend on your exact age in the financial year.
  • →Retirement & pension — EPF, NPS and annuity eligibility are age-linked.
  • →Insurance — life and health premiums are priced on your age at entry.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about age calculation

How is exact age calculated?
Age is calculated by finding the difference between the date of birth and today (or a specified date). Years are counted first, then remaining months, then remaining days. Importantly, the day count accounts for the varying lengths of months — a month of February has 28 or 29 days, not 30.
What is the legal age of majority in India?
In India, the legal age of majority is 18 years as per the Indian Majority Act. The legal age for marriage is 18 for women and 21 for men (proposed to be raised to 21 for both). The voting age is 18. The age for criminal liability is 18. Senior citizen benefits apply from age 60.
How are leap years handled in age calculation?
A leap year has 366 days (February has 29 days). If you were born on Feb 29, your birthday is celebrated on Feb 28 or Mar 1 in non-leap years (varies by country/person preference). Our calculator uses Feb 28 as the birthday in non-leap years for Feb 29 births. A year is a leap year if divisible by 4, except century years must be divisible by 400.
What are the important age milestones in India?
Key age milestones: 5 (school admission), 15 (minor driving permit in some states), 18 (voting, driving license, legal majority), 21 (marriage for men), 25 (car insurance benefits), 40 (regular health checks recommended), 55 (NPS annuity eligibility), 58 (EPF retirement), 60 (senior citizen benefits, higher FD rates).
How do I calculate my age for a government exam or form?
Exams calculate age as the number of completed years as on a fixed cut-off date (e.g. 1 August of the exam year), not today's date. Set that cut-off in the "As of date" field above. If you were born on 15 Jun 2000 and the cut-off is 1 Aug 2025, your age is 25 years and 1 month.
How many days old am I?
Your age in days is the total days between your date of birth and today, counting 366 days for each leap year. As a rough guide, every year adds about 365 days — so a 25-year-old is around 9,131 days old. The calculator above shows your exact day, week, and month totals.
What is the difference between age and completed years?
"Completed years" is the age you reached at your last birthday — the figure almost every Indian form and law uses. So someone who is 24 years and 11 months is officially "24", not 25. This calculator reports completed years plus the leftover months and days.
Can I calculate age between two past dates?
Yes. Enter the earlier date as the date of birth and the later date in the "As of date" field. The tool then shows the exact gap in years, months and days — handy for finding someone's age on a past event, or the duration between any two dates.
